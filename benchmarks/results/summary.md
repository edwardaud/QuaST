## Verdicts on the claims the audit flagged

**Annulus: is the 0.20 % agreement inside the uncertainty?** The
finest-rung observed order is p = 1.690 on Nu_T (1.594 on Nu_B), not the
nominal 2, so the sequence is still pre-asymptotic. GCI(Fs = 1.25) of the
finest Nu_T is **0.462 %** (0.342 % if p = 2 is assumed instead); the
headline deviation |+0.2035 %| is therefore *smaller* than the finest
rung's own discretization uncertainty (`|dev|/GCI = 0.44`).
**Verdict: the agreement is consistent with ASPECT, but it is not an
accuracy of 0.20 %** — the accuracy of the finest value is 0.34-0.46 %,
and 0.20 % is the deviation, not the error bar. Nu_B (+0.114 %
deviation, GCI 0.597 %) is comfortably inside its own uncertainty.

**Annulus: is the convergence second order?** No — the measured
finest-pair order is 1.69 (Nu_T) and 1.59 (Nu_B). The p = 2
extrapolation lands on the Firedrake value 9.5412, which supports the
second-order *assumption*, but it is an assumption, not a measured order;
the public page's "ratios 2.2, 3.6 and 5.0" are error ratios, not an
observed order, and a ratio of 5.0 corresponds to p ~ 2.3, i.e. outside
the asymptotic range (AUDIT_VV F2 confirmed).

**Blankenbach: is the box convergence second order?** Only case 1a is
in the asymptotic range: p = 1.999 (recorded) / 1.955 (this campaign),
GCI 0.083 % / 0.089 %. 1b measures p = 1.652 on the recorded L3-L5
ladder (GCI 0.560 %); this campaign re-ran L2-L4, whose observed order
is 0.48 with a 9.5 % GCI — i.e. the coarse end is still far from
asymptotic, and the recorded L3-L5 estimate is the usable one. 1c has
non-monotone differences on both the recorded and the new ladders, so
no observed order exists at all. The public page's "a factor of 4 a
second-order element predicts" is a plausibility statement; it is a
measurement only for 1a.

**Tosi case 1:** single resolution, no ladder exists, so no GCI is
quoted; the deviation from the 11-code median (+0.0068 % Nu_top,
-0.081 % Vrms) is a deviation, not an uncertainty.

**2a:** the published wall-refined rung is reproduced to about 3e-6
relative (Nu_top 10.124846 vs 10.124814, Vrms 479.6980 vs 479.6949).
The uniform L1-L4 sequence gives the resolution study and its finest
value's GCI is 3.3 % at p_obs = 1.27 (pre-asymptotic, as the 1e3
viscosity contrast suggests); the quoted wall-refined rung is a
different, non-uniform tree, so its own uncertainty cannot be derived
from this sequence — but the quoted +0.58 % deviation is well inside
the 3.3 % uniform-card discretization uncertainty.
